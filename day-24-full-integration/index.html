What are the class timings and time commitment?

Cohorts are structured for working professionals with live evening lectures (twice weekly) and weekend interactive build sessions, requiring roughly 8-12 hours per week.

Are payment installments or employer sponsorships supported?

Yes! We provide zero-interest split installment plans, ISA options for eligible domestic students, and formal invoices formatted for corporate tuition reimbursement.

Will I receive an industry-recognized certificate?

Upon satisfactory completion of all modules, code reviews, and the capstone project defense, you receive an accredited verifiable credential with a cryptographic hash.

What equipment or software is required?

A modern laptop (macOS, Windows 10/11, or Linux) with at least 8GB RAM, Git, Node.js LTS, and VS Code installed. All cloud credits and lab sandboxes are provided.
