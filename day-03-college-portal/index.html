Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the minimum attendance requirement for semester exams?

Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance across every registered theory course and 85% attendance in all laboratory practicals to qualify for end-semester examinations.

How do I register for the upcoming InnoByte hackathon?

Visit the Student Council office or register via the online portal using your student identification credentials before the deadline on September 20, 2026.

Where can students access online library journals and IEEE papers?

Digital subscriptions can be accessed through the campus Intranet proxy using your student email credentials at library.springfield.edu/portal.

Who should I contact if there is a scheduling clash in elective subjects?

Submit a formal change request to the Academic Registrar office located on the first floor of the administrative block before the close of add/drop week.
